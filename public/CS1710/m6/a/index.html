Prototype A · click to drill down

Click a cell, a country name, or a genre name. The two views on the right rebuild for that selection. Click the same thing again, or the button below, to go back to everything.

It answers one question at a time: where should a scout look, is that cell's edge bigger than chance, and was it earned broadly or by a couple of runaway hits?

How to read this

  • Blue dot — inside the 95% ellipse, within chance.
  • Orange dot — outside it, unusual on its own.
  • Outer ellipse — the 95% chance region.
  • Inner ellipse — the 68% region.
  • Diamond — whatever you have selected right now.
  • Cell colour — mean log-streams vs that cell's own genre.
  • Grey bar — how many tracks sit behind that row.
  • One seat — 1% of the selection's total streams.
