Prototype B · brush the ring, filter by year

Every point is one country × genre combination — 120 of them. Drag a rectangle on the ring to select a group of them; the heatmap keeps only those cells in colour. Drag inside the ring again without moving to clear. The year sliders rebuild both views.

It answers a question A cannot: do the statistically unusual cells cluster in a country or a genre, or are they scattered the way chance scatters things?

How to read this

  • Blue dot — inside the 95% ellipse, within chance.
  • Orange dot — outside it; about 6 of 120 are expected to be.
  • Outer ellipse — the 95% chance region.
  • Inner ellipse — the 68% region.
  • Drag a box — selects every point inside it.
  • Cell colour — mean log-streams vs that cell's own genre.
  • Grey bar — how many tracks sit behind that row.
  • One seat — 1% of the brushed set's total streams.
